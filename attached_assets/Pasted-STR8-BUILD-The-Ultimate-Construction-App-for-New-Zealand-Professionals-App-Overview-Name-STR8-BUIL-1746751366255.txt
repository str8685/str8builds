STR8 BUILD: The Ultimate Construction App for New Zealand Professionals
App Overview
Name: STR8 BUILD
Owner: C.SAMU
Version: 3.4 (as of May 9, 2025)
Target Market: New Zealand construction professionals
Primary Locations: Throughout New Zealand (e.g., Papamoa, Bay of Plenty)
App Vision
STR8 BUILD serves as the ultimate digital toolkit and job management assistant for New Zealand construction professionals. It enhances efficiency, accuracy, compliance, and business administration on every job site through a comprehensive suite of NZ-specific resources, powerful calculators, mobile sensor-based tools, supplier locator functionality, intelligent weather analysis, AI-powered assistance, and integrated timesheet/invoicing capabilities.

UI/UX Design
The app features a cutting-edge futuristic UI with:

Color Scheme: Deep space blues, dark purples, and near-blacks for backgrounds with electric blues, vibrant cyans, and energetic teals as accent colors
Visual Elements: Minimalist, sharp, geometric icons with neon/holographic styling
UI Components: Clean lines, smooth transitions, translucent/frosted glass effects, geometric patterns, and HUD-like overlays
Typography: Modern sans-serif fonts with stylized geometric/condensed fonts for headers and key readouts
Dynamic Elements: Subtle animated backgrounds on key screens
Interactive Widgets: Sleek modules with translucent backgrounds, glowing borders, and animated data displays
Core Features
1. Dashboard (Primary Home Screen)
A central hub providing at-a-glance information and quick actions:

Job Timer: Visual time tracking integrated into the dashboard
Quick Summaries: Customizable widgets displaying key information
Customizable Layout: User-configurable dashboard arrangement
2. Timesheet Management
Comprehensive time tracking and reporting system:

Automatic Logging & Manual Entry: Multiple input methods
Hourly Rate Tracking & Calculations: Accurate financial tracking
Reporting & Export: Formatted reports for accounting purposes
3. Invoicing & Quoting
Professional financial document creation and management:

Invoice & Quote Creation: Templates with editable fields
Automatic Calculations: NZD currency with GST integration
Management & Tracking: Status monitoring for all documents
Client Management: Organized client database
Customizable Company Details: Branding and contact information
4. NZ Building Resources
Digital library of construction reference materials:

Specialized Categories: Resources for stairs, roofing, decking, carpentry, GIB®, geometry, concrete, and fencing
Interactive Elements: Modern presentation of diagrams and compliance checklists
5. Mobile Tools Suite
Sensor-based utilities for on-site use:

Measurement Tools: Digital measuring capabilities
Level and Angle Tools: Precision alignment utilities
Sound Measurement: Noise level monitoring
Project Camera & Site Notes: Visual documentation system
Additional Tools: Various sensor-based utilities
6. NZ Suppliers Directory & Locator
Comprehensive building materials supplier database:

Location-Based Map View: GPS-centered supplier mapping
Nearby Suppliers List: Dynamic, sortable supplier information
Search & Filtering: Category-based supplier filtering
Detailed Supplier Profiles: Complete vendor information
Offline Access: Available for favorited suppliers
User Contributions: Community updates (Phase 2)
7. Weather Impact Calculator
Job site weather analysis:

Weather Data Visualization: Futuristic charts and graphs
Impact Analysis: Construction-specific weather interpretation
8. AI Integration (Gemini)
Intelligent assistant functionality:

Voice Commands: Hands-free operation
Smart Recommendations: Contextual suggestions
Seamless Interface: Fully integrated into the app's design language
9. Blocklayer.com Calculator Integration
Construction calculation tools:

Reskinned Interface: Maintaining consistent design
3D Visualizations: Interactive building component models
Navigation and Layout
Bottom Navigation Bar: 5 primary tabs (Dashboard, Tools, Calculators, Resources, Jobs & Admin)
Quick Access Features: Persistent AI search bar, floating action button, swipe gestures
Responsive Design: Optimized for both phones and tablets
Accessibility and Usability
Job Site Adaptations: Glove-friendly targets, high-contrast mode, voice commands, offline functionality
User Customization: Favorite tools, quick access settings, project-based configurations
PWA Installation: "Add to Home Screen" functionality
